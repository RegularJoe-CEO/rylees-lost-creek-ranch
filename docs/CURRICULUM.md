## Module 2 trail (22 September 2026)

The earlier paragraph applies to the six original place-value chapters. For the new measurement trail, the [Bluebonnet Learning Grade 4 Module 2 Teacher Edition, Edition 1](https://d1yqpar94jqbqm.cloudfront.net/documents/BLMath_Gr4_M2_TE_ENG.pdf) was inspected directly. Its module overview, PDF page 10 (printed page 7), lists Topics A–C and eight lesson objectives. The publicly posted [Grade 4 Course Guide's Module 2 Tips for Families](https://files-backend.assets.thrillshare.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/5591/Jisd/ccc5b294-711f-4744-8289-5e2c3dac4e89/4th-Grade-Module-2-English.pdf?disposition=inline) confirms length, mass, capacity, conversions, multi-step word problems, and input-output patterns.

| Game trail | Published lesson objective, summarized in our words | Implementation scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson 1 | Convert metric length to smaller units; solve length addition and subtraction | Original kilometer/meter mixed conversions and stories; not the entire lesson. |
| Lesson 2 | Convert metric mass to smaller units; solve mass addition and subtraction | Original kilogram/gram conversions and stories; not the entire lesson. |
| Lesson 3 | Convert metric capacity to smaller units; solve capacity addition and subtraction | Original liter/milliliter conversions and stories; not the entire lesson. |
| Lesson 4 | Relate metric units to place value; express measurements in different units | Compare and rename compatible units. |
| Lesson 5 | Solve multi-step metric length, mass, and capacity problems | Original two-step problems using a common smaller unit. |
| Lessons 6–8 | Use input-output tables, expressions, and number patterns | Generate and find additive, subtractive, and multiplicative input-output rules. No claim of full lesson coverage. |
| Ahead warm-up | Module 3 begins multi-digit multiplication and division | Original multiplication warm-up only; it is **not** labeled as an official Module 3 lesson. |

The game does **not** reproduce Succeed's text, problems, or images. Parent chooses the current school lesson from 1–8. The earlier place-value chapters remain available to review; the full Module 2 trail and multiplication warm-up are accessible in order to explore ahead. Since the current page in Rylee's copy is unknown, this is a flexible window, not a claim of exactly 2–3 instructional weeks of lesson-complete coverage. The Grade 4 Teacher Edition describes 3 days in Topic A, 2 in Topic B, 3 in Topic C, plus an assessment. School pacing can differ. The game is supplemental practice, not a replacement for instruction or the book.
